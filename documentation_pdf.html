Lyfpartner

Voice-First, Multilingual Autonomous Banking Hotline for 35M+ Excluded Nigerians

🎯 The Exclusion Gap

Over 35 million Nigerians living with visual impairments or low digital literacy are excluded by text-heavy USSD menus and visually demanding mobile apps. Customers are forced to share PINs with third parties, creating massive fraud vulnerability.

💡 The Autonomous Voice Solution

A zero-interface, toll-free banking hotline (333). Customers dial in, speak naturally in Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, or Nigerian English, receive warm female conversational voice feedback, and conduct banking with zero screens.

1. System Architecture & Component Flow

Lyfpartner couples real-time telephony stream decoding with Groq-accelerated AI models and a deterministic NIP banking core.

VOICE ACTIVITY DETECTION
Hands-Free VAD

Continuous client-side Web Audio AnalyserNode tracks room ambient sound and auto-detects speech completion after 950ms of silence. No buttons needed.

ASR & TRANSCRIPTION
Groq Whisper Large v3

Ultra-low latency speech transcription. Incoming Opus/WebM audio is piped directly through ffmpeg into 16kHz mono WAV for <300ms transcription.

NEURAL TTS
Native 24kHz Audio

Microsoft Edge Neural TTS (en-NG-EzinneNeural) with rate=+8% encoded at native 24kHz PCM16 WAV. Eliminates slow-motion pitch distortion.

4. Core Banking State Machine & Security

Deterministic Financial Guards

While conversational chit-chat is powered by Groq LLM, all financial balances, debit amounts, and PIN checks run through hardcoded deterministic Python controllers. An LLM cannot hallucinate or transfer unauthorized funds.

Two-Step Verbal Confirmation

Sensitive monetary transactions require an explicit quote step and verbal affirmation ("Yes", "Confirm", "Proceed", "Oya") before the ledger creates an irreversible NIP transfer reference.
